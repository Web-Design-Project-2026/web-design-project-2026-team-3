Between the Lines

Between the Lines is an event for all book lovers with good vibes, good books and good activities. It is a great opportunity to meet new people, to talk about your favourite books, discover new genres and to maybe even meet your favourite authors, as we have some writers as guests talking about their books. You can also buy books during the event. Come and bring all your friends as well and have a great time!
